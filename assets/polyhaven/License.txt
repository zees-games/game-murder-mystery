Textures from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com) and ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com), both released
under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain): https://polyhaven.com/license and https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/

Each was downloaded at 1K, resized to 512 x 512 and saved as JPEG: <id>.jpg is the diffuse (albedo)
map and <id>_n.jpg the OpenGL-convention normal map. Where a level or saturation change is noted the
photograph was darker or duller than a lit room wants; nothing else was altered. The ids are the generic
material ids of dot-map's tools/bsp/dotbsp/source_textures.tsv, drawn by game/mm_materials.gd.
