# Furniture models

Forty-six of the furniture models are this game's own, built from Kenney's Furniture Kit by `art-src/build_props.py` (Blender in background mode: `blender -b -P art-src/build_props.py`). The rest of the folder is the kit as Kenney made it.

- **Base**: [Kenney's Furniture Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit) (CC0 1.0; licence in `LICENSE-kenney-furniture-kit.txt`). The unmodified originals are kept in `art-src/base/`.
- **Rebuilt**: `pottedPlant`, `chairCushion`, `kitchenBar`, `desk`, `chairDesk`, `bookcaseOpen`, `loungeChair`, `cardboardBoxClosed`, `cabinetBedDrawerTable`, `trashcan`.
- **Rebuilt, second pass** (the next twelve most used across the maps): `rugDoormat`, `bedSingle`, `bookcaseClosed`, `stoolBar`, `lampRoundTable`, `toilet`, `bathroomSink`, `cabinetTelevisionDoors`, `televisionModern`, `table`, `showerRound`, `chair`. Same palette; new materials porcelain, linen, lampshade and screen. Modelled onto them: a trim band and finials on the bed, shelved books and a cornice on the closed bookcase, brass rim and rubber feet on the stool, a brass base and rings on the lamp, a dark seat and flush button on the toilet, taps on the basin, brass pulls on the television cabinet, an indicator light on the screen, a velvet seat on the chair. The shower's glass is left as it was. Sheets in `reports/models/2026-10-20/props/`.
- **Rebuilt, third pass** (the next twelve by map use): `sideTableDrawers`, `cabinetBedDrawer`, `bookcaseClosedWide`, `loungeSofa`, `coatRackStanding`, `bench`, `plantSmall2`, `cardboardBoxOpen`, `bookcaseOpenLow`, `computerScreen`, `lampRoundFloor`, `lampSquareFloor`. Same palette plus a dim green for the screen's text lines. Modelled onto them: brass knobs and a book or blotter on the tables, shelved books and a plinth on the bookcases, teal leather and two cushions and brass feet on the sofa, brass hooks and finial on the coat rack, a velvet seat on the bench, a rim and soil and leaves on the small plant, tape and a label on the open carton, an inset screen with lines of text on the monitor, brass rings and a base on the floor lamps.
- **Rebuilt, fourth pass** (the next twelve by map use): `books`, `kitchenCabinet`, `chairRounded`, `sideTable`, `computerKeyboard`, `bookcaseClosedDoors`, `cabinetBed`, `kitchenFridgeLarge`, `speaker`, `tableRound`, `loungeChairRelax`, `toaster`. Same Kenney Furniture Kit base (https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit, Kenney, CC0 1.0) and palette. Modelled onto them: brass bookend and gilt bands on the books, a slate top, plinth, brass rail and a hung towel on the kitchen cabinet, a velvet seat on the chair, a leather inlay and shelf books on the side table, keys on the keyboard, panelled doors with brass pulls on the bookcase, a leather top and knob on the bedside cabinet, vents, handles and a plinth on the fridge, woofers, tweeter and plinth on the speaker, a leather top, brass rim and foot on the round table, a pillow on the lounge chair, slots, lever and dial on the toaster. Sheets in `reports/models/2026-10-28/props/`.
- **Rebuilt, fifth pass** (beds, sofas, rugs, kitchen and small items from the mansion rooms): `bedDouble`, `bedBunk`, `loungeSofaLong`, `loungeDesignSofa`, `rugRectangle`, `rugRound`, `lampSquareTable`, `televisionVintage`, `plantSmall1`, `kitchenStove`, `kitchenSink`, `kitchenMicrowave`, `tableCoffee`. Same Kenney Furniture Kit base (https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit, Kenney, CC0 1.0) and palette. Modelled onto them: brass trim, linen turn-down and paper-topped pillows on the beds, two cushions on each sofa, brass borders and a cross or rings on the rugs, a base and band on the lamp, a screen, dials and feet on the old television, a rim and soil on the plant, hob rings, rail, dials and plinth on the stove, a plinth, slate edge and towel on the sink, dials and a light on the microwave, a leather inlay and brass rails on the coffee table. Sheets in `reports/models/2026-10-28/props/` (`contact_c.png`, `contact_d.png`).
- **Changes**: every sharp edge is bevelled; the kit's pale pine, coral and silver are replaced by stained walnut, burgundy velvet, brass and gunmetal. Modelled onto them: shelved books on the bookcase, a leather blotter and brass pull on the desk, a slate top and brass rail on the kitchen island, a soil disc and rim on the pot, a taped and labelled carton, a book and brass dish on the bedside table, a brass rail cap on the chair, armrest pads on the desk chair, a pillow on the lounge chair, brass rim and ribs on the bin.
- **Kept**: node names and tree, origin, and the bounding box (each model is refitted to the stock box, so `props/catalogue.json` and the collision in `props/pieces.json` are unchanged). The material named `plant` keeps its name because the loader's recolour table finds it by name.
- Contact sheets (stock, three-quarter, front, side; Cycles) are in `reports/models/2026-10-19/props/`; the third pass's (stock behind, rebuilt in front, `art-src/render_grid.py`) in `reports/models/2026-10-27/props/`.
